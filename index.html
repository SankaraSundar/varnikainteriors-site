Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical full home interior project take?

Our standard residential interior projects (2BHK / 3BHK) are completed in 30 to 45 working days from 3D design approval and material sign-off.

Do you provide 3D designs before starting factory execution?

Yes! We provide complete 3D realistic visualizations and 2D working drawings so you can visualize every color, texture, and dimension prior to execution.

What warranty do you offer on modular furniture?

We provide up to a 10-year warranty on boiling water-resistant (BWR/BWP) plywood and lifetime warranties on premium hardware brands like Häfele, Blum, and Ebco.

Can I customize the interior budget according to my preference?

Absolutely! We tailor every project specifically around your budget without compromising on structural quality or design aesthetics.
